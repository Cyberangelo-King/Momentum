## [1.0.0] - 2026-08-27 — Genesis Release: Governed Evolutionary Intelligence Architecture

### Core Architecture & Governance
- **Momentum Constitution:** Enacted the 17 Immutable Laws guaranteeing User Agency, Truth, Security Supremacy, Safety Veto, and Human Oversight.
- **5 Security Zones:** Isolated Zone 0 (Governance), Zone 1 (Control Plane), Zone 2 (Intelligence Plane), Zone 3 (Data Plane), and Zone 4 (Sandbox).

### Intelligence Subsystems
- **Perception & Event Bus:** Validated, signed event ingestion pipeline with anomaly detection and context aggregation.
- **Tiered Memory Store:** Episodic, Semantic, Product, and World Memory with strict data minimization policies.
- **Contextual Trust Engine:** Domain-segmented trust graph with Sybil attack resistance and collusion defenses.
- **Reasoning & Prediction:** Outcome forecaster with explicit uncertainty bounds and prediction error tracking ($-\log P$).
- **Curiosity & Hypothesis Engine:** Information-gain driven hypothesis synthesis (`PROPOSED`, `TESTING`, `SUPPORTED`, `REJECTED`).
- **Controlled Experimentation:** Safe A/B & Champion/Challenger testing engine with cohort segmentation.
- **Evolutionary Engine:** Governed candidate generator, multi-objective fitness evaluation, and lineage ledger.
- **Capability Graph & World Model:** Feature dependency DAG, opportunity discovery, and unmet need detector.
- **Fail-Safe Deployment:** Emergency kill switches (`KILL_SWITCH_GLOBAL`, `KILL_SWITCH_EVOLUTION`, etc.) and automated rollback triggers.
