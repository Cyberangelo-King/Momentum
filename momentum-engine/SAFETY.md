# Momentum Safety & Human Welfare Architecture

## 1. Principles of Healthy Engagement

Momentum is built to maximize **Real Human Value & High-Leverage Relationship Capital**, explicitly rejecting dark patterns, addictive feedback loops, and psychological manipulation.

### Prohibited Optimization Targets
- ❌ Continuous endless scrolling / time-on-screen maximization
- ❌ Artificial urgency, fake FOMO, or deceptive notification schedules
- ❌ Emotional polarization or engagement-baiting controversy
- ❌ Concealed monetization or undisclosed algorithmic bias

### Mandated Optimization Targets
- ✅ High-value connection conversion rate (real-world conversations & follow-ups)
- ✅ User agency and intentional session conclusion
- ✅ Transparent reasoning and explainable recommendations
- ✅ Accurate uncertainty signaling

---

## 2. Safety Evaluation Matrix

Before any candidate strategy or experiment receives approval:
1. **Psychological Risk Score (< 0.05 threshold):** Assesses notification frequency, urgency language, and deceptive prompts.
2. **Harm Detection (Zero Tolerance):** Scans for harassment patterns, unauthorized surveillance, or abusive content.
3. **Equitable Distribution Score (> 0.85):** Ensures recommendations do not disproportionately starve emerging speakers or attendees.
